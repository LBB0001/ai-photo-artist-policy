This Privacy Policy describes how AI Photo Artist ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Prahant Labs, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our application on Android and iOS devices. By using AI Photo Artist, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1 Information We Collect

1.1 Photos and Images

When you use the Style Transform, Object Eraser, or Face Swap features, you grant the app temporary access to photos you explicitly select from your device gallery or capture via your camera. These photos are used exclusively to perform the requested AI transformation. To do so, they are uploaded to the third-party AI processing providers described in Section 3. We operate no servers of our own and retain no copy of your photos.

1.3 Technical Usage Data

We do not collect analytics, crash logs, device identifiers, IP addresses, or behavioral data through our own systems. Their data handling is governed by their own respective privacy policies.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To perform the AI style transformation, image generation, or object erasing operation you explicitly requested.
  • To temporarily display the generated result image on your device screen.
  • To allow you to optionally save the result to your device's native photo gallery.
  • To remember your UI preferences (theme and language) across app sessions.
  • To provide Help, FAQ, and legal documentation within the app.

We do NOT use your photos or data to train AI models, create advertising profiles, sell to third parties, or for any purpose not explicitly described in this policy.

3 Data Sharing and Third Parties

Third-Party AI Processing Services

When you request an AI-powered style transformation or image generation, the image and/or text prompt you provide is transmitted to one of the following third-party GPU cloud processing services:

  • Fal.ai (fal.ai) – Image-to-image style transfer and FLUX generative models. Review their Privacy Policy at fal.ai/privacy.
  • Hugging Face Inference API (huggingface.co) – AI model inference routing. Review their Privacy Policy at huggingface.co/privacy.
  • Pollinations (pollinations.ai) – Fallback image generation used when the services above are unavailable.

These services process your submitted image data solely to perform the AI computation and return the result. We do not control their data retention practices and recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics companies, social networks, or any other third party beyond the AI processing services described above.

5 Data Retention and Deletion

  • Photos you select for processing are uploaded to the third-party AI providers listed in Section 3 in order to perform the requested transformation. Depending on the provider, an uploaded image may be stored on that provider's servers or content delivery network for the duration of, and for a period after, the processing request. Retention of that copy is governed by the provider's own policy, not by us. We operate no servers of our own and keep no copy of your photos.
  • Generated artwork images are stored locally on your device only and are fully under your control.
  • You can delete individual creations from the in-app Gallery at any time using the Delete button.
  • You can clear all locally cached data, prompt history, and settings by tapping "Clear Cache" in Settings, or by uninstalling the application.
  • There are no server-side user accounts, so there is no additional data deletion request process required.

7 Data Security

  • All network communications between the app and third-party AI processing services use industry-standard TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Your optional Fal.ai API key is stored using platform-level encrypted secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • All locally stored creations and preferences are stored in app-sandboxed storage, inaccessible to other apps on your device.
